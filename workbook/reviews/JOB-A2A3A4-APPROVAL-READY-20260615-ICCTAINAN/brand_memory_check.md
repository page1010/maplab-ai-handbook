## MAPLAB Brand Value

MAPLAB Kitchen should read as natural, warm, quiet, detailed, stable, professional, and measured. The brand should not compete on cheapness or hard-selling.

For this 大臺南會展中心 plan, the market position is:

- Enterprise event table planning.
- Clean presentation and smooth guest flow.
- Brand reception and host-side dignity.
- Venue-aware catering for meetings, exhibitions, openings, presentations, and VIP reception.

## Brand Voice

Use scene-first language:

- Speak about event flow, guest movement, table placement, photo moments, and reception rhythm.
- Prefer concrete nouns: `會議茶點`, `展覽接待`, `開幕茶會`, `貴賓點心吧`, `品牌發表會餐點`.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, price-first language, and competitor comparison.
- Public copy should not print internal strategy notes.
