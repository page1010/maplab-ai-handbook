## Important Routing Rule

Strategy owner owns the loop. Shared surface owner only owns cross-strategy
renderer/readability defects.

Examples:

- 網紅雷達 output bad -> IOS-KOL
- 漲停/動能 Top3 stale -> IOS-MOMENTUM
- 實單風險 unclear -> IOS-INVENTORY
- Polymarket/noisy alpha -> IOS-ALPHA or IOS-BLACKSWAN
- Dirty worktree keep/drop -> IOS-HYGIENE
- Multi-strategy card/Telegram formatting broken -> IOS-SURFACE
