# Suno subscription rights checkpoint

## Official-source finding

- Suno states that subscribing later does not grant retroactive commercial rights to songs created on the free plan by default.
- Suno states that songs created while subscribed to Pro or Premier receive commercial use rights, and those rights are retained after cancellation.
- Commercial-use rights do not guarantee copyright protection; copyright eligibility depends on jurisdiction and human contribution.

Official references:

- https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2425729
- https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2410177
- https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2416769
- https://help.suno.com/en/articles/9601665
