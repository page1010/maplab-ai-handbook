## 5 Key Factors Affecting Catering Prices in Tainan  

### 1. Event Size and Venue Type  
Headcount is the most direct factor influencing catering costs. Generally, the larger the headcount, the greater the chance the per‑person unit price will decrease because overall planning space is larger. However, small private parties (such as 10–20‑person birthdays or first‑birthday celebrations) tend to have higher customization, which may push the per‑person unit price slightly up. Venue conditions also affect pricing—if extra moving is required or basic water/electricity facilities are lacking, additional fees may arise.  

### 2. Menu Content and Cuisine Style  
Catering menus range from simple lunch boxes to refined Western finger food, dessert tables, and varied dish selections, leading to large price differences. Choosing cold platters and handmade pastries usually costs less than options requiring on‑site hot food preparation. MAPLAB Kitchen offers multiple style combinations; menu content directly reflects in the quotation.  

### 3. Service Scope and On‑site Requirements  
MAPLAB Kitchen’s standard quotation includes **food preparation and table setup arrangement**. If any of the following extra needs exist, they will be quoted separately: backdrop production, lighting & sound, MC, tableside service, special on‑site service, custom drink mixing, etc. Please mention these during consultation so we can provide a transparent, complete quotation.  

### 4. Special Requirements and Customization Level  
If your event has special requests—such as brand logo output, theme color coordination, specific dietary restrictions (vegetarian, allergen‑free), or needing to match venue equipment—these will affect the final quotation. The higher the customization level, the more preparation time and cost involved. We recommend discussing special needs early with us to allow sufficient time for planning the most suitable solution.  

### 5. Event Timing and Booking Timeline  
Weekends and peak seasons (Christmas, year‑end banquet period, wedding season) see high demand; we suggest booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance. Early booking secures your date and gives us more time to adjust to your needs, sometimes even allowing better ingredient procurement arrangements.

## Common Misconceptions About Catering Pricing  

### Misconception 1: “Catering is always cheaper than dining at a restaurant”  
Many assume catering merely means “delivering food,” so it should be cheaper than eating at a restaurant. In reality, catering’s cost structure is completely different—it includes ingredients, preparation, transportation, on‑site service, equipment, etc., each a professional investment. The value of catering lies in “delivering a complete dining experience to your chosen location,” which is fundamentally different from restaurant banquet service.  

### Misconception 2: “Fewer guests always mean higher cost”  
Small parties (10–20 people) do have a slightly higher per‑person cost, but this does not mean small‑scale catering is not worthwhile. Often, a carefully prepared 15‑person first‑birthday party costs under 20,000 TWD total, yet creates a warmer, more memorable experience than a restaurant banquet.  

### Misconception 3: “Online prices are the final price”  
Responsible caterers never post a “final price” online because each event’s needs differ. Online figures are usually reference ranges or starting prices. The true quotation is confirmed only after discussing your requirements—this is why we advise early consultation to avoid budget gaps.
