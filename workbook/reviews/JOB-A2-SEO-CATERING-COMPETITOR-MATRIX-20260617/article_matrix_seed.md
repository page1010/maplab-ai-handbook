## Drafting Rules

- Do not copy competitor copy.
- Treat competitor pages as architecture references only.
- Keep MAPLAB tone: warm, precise, visual, reliable, not discount-driven.
- Every factual claim must be labeled:
  - `verified_public`
  - `verified_internal`
  - `reasonable_inference`
  - `needs_owner_evidence`
- Every article must include:
  - search intent
  - target keyword
  - supporting keywords
  - H1 / SEO title / meta description
  - outline
  - FAQ section
  - image/asset needs
  - internal links to verify
  - publication risk notes
