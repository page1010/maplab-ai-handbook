# Tainan Picnic Box Recommendation: Essential Takeaway Light Food Combination  

On a sunny afternoon, sitting on the ground with family and friends to share food is a picnic scene many people long for. However, once you start preparing, you’ll find it’s more complicated than imagined—prepping ingredients, packing, keeping fresh, portioning, transporting… layer after layer of tedious chores often discourages people.  

If you wish to complete an outdoor gathering more easily, using a ready-made picnic box is a good option.  

## How to choose a suitable picnic box? Three suggestions for your reference  

- **When is a multi‑compartment party box suitable for outdoor takeaway?** It is recommended to choose a multi‑compartment party box for **6 people or more**, making sharing easier and food distribution and visual presentation richer.  
- **If the venue lacks power or shelter, what should you do?** It is recommended to focus on cold foods and ensure they are not left at room temperature for more than **2 hours** to maintain freshness and safety.  
- **Given Tainan’s high temperatures, how to choose more durable items?** It is advised to avoid mousse‑type and refrigerated desserts, and instead opt for savory snacks, dry‑style desserts, or room‑temperature stable items, and finish them in the same meal.  

## How to choose picnic box contents based on different event themes?  
For proposals, birthdays, etc., you can choose a dessert box or cheese platter; for group activities, a meat or salad combination that provides satiety is suitable.

- **Custom Salad Set:** Hydroponic lettuce + smoked chicken breast/salmon/nuts + chef’s special dressing – suitable for health‑oriented or low‑carb diners.
