# Foreign Catering SEO Keyword Matrix Benchmark

Date: 2026-06-17

## Executive Read

The strongest benchmark is Social Pantry. It combines luxury positioning, service pages, sub-service pages, venue/location pages, case studies, "catering for" taxonomy, and blog/guide content in a Yoast sitemap structure. This is the closest architecture to what MAPLAB needs for Tainan high-quality catering: not one giant article, but a repeatable matrix that turns services, locations, venues, buyer roles, and proof cases into internal-linkable pages.

ZeroCater is the strongest pure matrix architecture benchmark. Its sitemap exposes a scaled SEO system across vendor states/cities, cuisine types, dietary restrictions, menu templates, customer pages, studies, comparison pages, and blog content. MAPLAB should not copy the marketplace model, but the taxonomy logic is useful.

Fooditude is the best focused B2B office catering benchmark. It targets London office/corporate lunch and event-catering intent with a tighter service matrix, supported by corporate credibility as part of Sodexo Group.

Rocket Food is the best premium visual/case-study benchmark. Its matrix is less keyword-explicit than Social Pantry or ZeroCater, but its case-study and venue proof layer is useful for MAPLAB's premium positioning.

## Evidence Sources

### 1. Social Pantry

- Homepage: https://socialpantry.co.uk/
- Sitemap index: https://socialpantry.co.uk/sitemap_index.xml
- Service sitemap: https://socialpantry.co.uk/we_offer-sitemap.xml

Observed SEO structure:

- Homepage title targets luxury event, wedding, workplace, and London catering.
- Yoast sitemap exposes separate sitemap groups for posts, pages, services, venues, case studies, catering-for pages, locations, and categories.
- Service URLs include `weddings`, `catering`, `lunch-catering-services`, `private-catering`, `workplace`, `events`, `contract-catering-packages`, `canapes-catering-in-london`, `festive-canapes-catering-london`, `bbq-catering`, `garden-marquee-wedding-catering`, and `christmas-catering`.
- Blog/support URLs include event-catering education, contract-catering explainers, venue guides, and party-planning topics.
- Public schema on the homepage presents organization details including founding date, London address, founder, number of employees, and awards.

MAPLAB implication:

Use Social Pantry as the primary content-architecture benchmark. Build one pillar per revenue route, then support it with sub-service, venue, location, and case pages.

### 2. ZeroCater

- Homepage: https://zerocater.com/
- Sitemap index: https://zerocater.com/sitemap.xml

Observed SEO structure:

- Sitemap groups expose a large keyword matrix: posts, pages, press, customers, studies, comparison pages, vendors, vendor states, vendor cities, cuisine types, dietary restrictions, and menu templates.
- The model is not just "blog SEO"; it is a database-like SEO architecture where every practical buyer filter can become a landing page.

MAPLAB implication:

Use ZeroCater as the scale reference. MAPLAB does not need thousands of pages now, but should design the matrix so future pages can be generated consistently from structured inputs: city, venue, event type, buyer role, menu type, proof asset, and FAQ.

### 3. Fooditude

- Homepage: https://www.fooditude.com/
- Sitemap: https://www.fooditude.com/sitemap.xml

Observed SEO structure:

- Homepage title targets corporate catering for London offices.
- Public schema identifies LocalBusiness positioning, London service area, and cuisine/service labels including corporate catering, office catering, and business lunch catering.
- Navigation/service pages include office catering, food programmes, one-off office lunch, corporate event catering in London, snacks, business lunch catering London, sustainable event catering London, and corporate catering in London.
- Site also maintains blog/insights and an office catering guide.

MAPLAB implication:

Use Fooditude as the B2B-office cluster model: office lunch, business lunch, meeting refreshment, sustainable/event catering, and guide content should each have separate pages or article clusters instead of being buried in one "corporate catering" page.

### 4. Rocket Food

- Homepage: https://www.rocketfood.com/
- Sitemap: https://www.rocketfood.com/sitemap.xml

Observed SEO structure:

- Homepage metadata targets London's top luxury caterer for events and weddings.
- Navigation emphasizes events, venues, case studies, sustainability, articles, workplace catering, sports hospitality, and food/drink.
- Sitemap includes high-profile case-study pages and service pages such as workplace catering, sports hospitality, and multiday events.

MAPLAB implication:

Use Rocket Food as the premium proof-layer benchmark. MAPLAB's venue/case pages should feel like proof of taste, trust, and execution, not just keyword pages.

## Recommended A2 Direction

Build MAPLAB's SEO matrix in this order:

1. Venue/location cluster: ICC Tainan, Tainan Convention Center, South Taiwan venues, Nanke, Anping, East District, Cheng Kung University area, Tainan Art Museum area.
2. B2B event cluster: corporate catering, meeting refreshments, seminar catering, press conference tea table, opening tea party, VIP reception, brand event catering.
3. Buyer-role cluster: admin/HR, marketing/PR agency, venue planner, founder/assistant, government or institution contact.
4. Menu-intent cluster: tea break, lunch box/lunch catering, canapes/finger food, dessert table, coffee break, vegetarian-friendly and allergen-aware options.
5. Proof cluster: real case pages, venue guides, event-flow checklists, FAQ/pricing explainers.

This should be staged as a matrix, not a one-off article sprint.
